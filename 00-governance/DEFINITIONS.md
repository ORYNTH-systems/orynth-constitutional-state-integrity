# Definitions

FOUNDATIONAL SOURCE:
Canonical constitutional text.

DERIVED AUTHORITY:
A later authority object interpreting, implementing, applying, or constraining
a foundational source.

PREDICATE:
A condition whose resolution contributes to a legal or execution state.

AUTHORITY:
Permission validly possessed by an actor or institution within defined scope.

JURISDICTION:
The subject, actor, territorial, temporal, and remedial boundary of authority.

RELEVANCE:
A dependency relationship indicating that resolution of one state bears upon
another state.

ALTERATION:
An authorized consequential change to a state.

FINALITY:
A state in which defined forms of further consequential alteration are closed
or constrained.

AGENT:
A software or autonomous execution entity capable of acting within a system.

PRINCIPAL:
The authority-bearing human or institution from which valid delegation may
originate.
