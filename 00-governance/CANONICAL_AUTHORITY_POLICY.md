# Canonical Authority Policy

Authority classes are independently typed.

Minimum classes:

- FOUNDATIONAL_CONSTITUTIONAL
- AMENDATORY_CONSTITUTIONAL
- LEGISLATIVE
- JUDICIAL
- EXECUTIVE
- ADMINISTRATIVE
- STATE
- LOCAL
- DELEGATED
- AGENTIC_EXECUTION
- PROCEDURAL
- EVIDENTIARY

Authority rank and authority scope are separate variables.

Higher institutional rank does not itself establish predicate equivalence.

Execution capability does not establish authority.
