# CSI Constitutional Integrity Rule

The United States Constitution is the canonical foundational source for this
architecture.

ORYNTH CSI SHALL NOT:

1. rewrite constitutional source;
2. silently substitute interpretation for source;
3. infer foundational authority from institutional age;
4. treat implementation practice as constitutional text;
5. permit autonomous capability to create constitutional authority;
6. permit derived authority to overwrite its source lineage.

ORYNTH CSI MAY:

1. encode constitutional provisions as typed references;
2. map dependencies between constitutional provisions;
3. represent later interpretations as separately typed derived objects;
4. resolve jurisdiction and authority relationships;
5. evaluate predicate relationships;
6. trace constitutional meaning into consequential execution;
7. represent human, agentic, and hybrid execution environments;
8. identify retrospective and prospective deterministic relevance.

Every derived state must remain traceable to its originating source.
