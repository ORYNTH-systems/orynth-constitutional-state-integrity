# Source Precedence Policy

The architecture preserves source identity before evaluating derived authority.

Canonical source classes:

1. Constitution
2. Ratified Amendments

Derived authority classes are recorded separately and must preserve explicit
lineage to the source proposition they interpret, implement, constrain, or
apply.

Source precedence SHALL NOT be used as a shortcut for determining whether two
different predicates are equivalent.

Predicate equivalence requires an explicit equivalence determination.
