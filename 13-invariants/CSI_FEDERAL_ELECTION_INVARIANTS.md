# CSI Federal Election Invariants — Batch 05

CSI-FE-INV-001
Popular voter status and presidential elector status are independently typed.

CSI-FE-INV-002
Elector appointment authority does not create general voter qualification authority.

CSI-FE-INV-003
Candidate qualification and voter qualification remain independently typed.

CSI-FE-INV-004
Certificate custody does not establish counting authority.

CSI-FE-INV-005
Counting authority does not establish certification authority.

CSI-FE-INV-006
Election administration authority must resolve by operation.

CSI-FE-INV-007
Federal and state election authority must remain jurisdictionally typed.

CSI-FE-INV-008
Temporary appointment authority does not become permanent election authority.

CSI-FE-INV-009
Temporal office boundaries constrain authority state.

CSI-FE-INV-010
An expired term cannot continue merely because an execution system continues functioning.

CSI-FE-INV-011
Succession authority is event-bound.

CSI-FE-INV-012
Agentic execution cannot trigger constitutional succession by self-authorization.

CSI-FE-INV-013
Delegation cannot expand election authority.

CSI-FE-INV-014
Delegation cannot expand election jurisdiction.

CSI-FE-INV-015
Registration removal requires predicate, evidence, process, and authority.

CSI-FE-INV-016
Ballot rejection requires predicate, evidence, process, and authority.

CSI-FE-INV-017
Tabulation effect requires ballot admission.

CSI-FE-INV-018
Certification requires operation-specific authority.

CSI-FE-INV-019
No software confidence score is itself a constitutional qualification.

CSI-FE-INV-020
Finality preserves authority and evidence lineage.
