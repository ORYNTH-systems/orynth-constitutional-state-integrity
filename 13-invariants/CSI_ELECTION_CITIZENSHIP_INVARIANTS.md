# CSI Election / Citizenship Invariants — Batch 04

CSI-EC-INV-001
Voter qualification and election administration remain independently typed.

CSI-EC-INV-002
Administrative authority does not create voter qualification.

CSI-EC-INV-003
Identity verification supports eligibility resolution but is not itself eligibility.

CSI-EC-INV-004
Evidence is not authority.

CSI-EC-INV-005
Database control is not jurisdiction.

CSI-EC-INV-006
Protected constitutional classifications cannot be silently transformed into exclusion predicates.

CSI-EC-INV-007
Valid voter-integrity controls must protect against both unauthorized admission and unauthorized exclusion.

CSI-EC-INV-008
Congressional enforcement authority remains derived from its constitutional enforcement source.

CSI-EC-INV-009
Fourteenth Amendment citizenship predicates remain independently traceable to Section 1 source.

CSI-EC-INV-010
A later citizenship interpretation may not overwrite the Section 1 source object.

CSI-EC-INV-011
Prior precedent does not itself prove predicate identity.

CSI-EC-INV-012
A newly relevant historical citizenship state is not automatically alterable.

CSI-EC-INV-013
Agentic election capability does not create election authority.

CSI-EC-INV-014
An autonomous system may detect conflict without acquiring adjudicatory authority.

CSI-EC-INV-015
Certification is a consequential transition requiring identifiable authority.

CSI-EC-INV-016
Finality preserves constitutional and evidentiary provenance.
