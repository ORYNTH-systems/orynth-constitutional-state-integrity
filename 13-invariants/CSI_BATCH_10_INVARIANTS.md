# CSI Batch 10 Invariants

CSI-B10-INV-001
Authority is temporally bounded.

CSI-B10-INV-002
Expired authority cannot execute consequential transitions.

CSI-B10-INV-003
Software continuity cannot extend expired authority.

CSI-B10-INV-004
Institutional continuity cannot extend expired authority.

CSI-B10-INV-005
Multiple apparently valid authorities do not collapse automatically into one.

CSI-B10-INV-006
Authority collisions remain visible until resolved through an authorized basis.

CSI-B10-INV-007
Capability cannot resolve authority collision.

CSI-B10-INV-008
Finality does not erase provenance.

CSI-B10-INV-009
Finality does not erase deterministic relevance.

CSI-B10-INV-010
Deterministic relevance does not automatically reopen final state.

CSI-B10-INV-011
Correctness does not automatically reopen final state.

CSI-B10-INV-012
New evidence does not automatically reopen final state.

CSI-B10-INV-013
Reopening requires authority and recognized process.

CSI-B10-INV-014
Prior finality state remains preserved after reopening.

CSI-B10-INV-015
Inherited-state invalidity does not self-authorize correction.

CSI-B10-INV-016
Live execution remains capable of producing a complete audit bundle.
