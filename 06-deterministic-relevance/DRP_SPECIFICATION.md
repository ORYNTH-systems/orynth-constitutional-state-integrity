# Deterministic Relevance Propagation — DRP

## Definition

When predicate P is resolved, CSI identifies every state whose validity,
admissibility, authority, evidence condition, or consequence depends upon P.

Two dependency directions exist.

RETROSPECTIVE:
States produced before the resolution point.

PROSPECTIVE:
States capable of being produced after the resolution point.

## Fundamental separation

RELEVANCE != AUTHORITY

RELEVANCE != ALTERATION

RELEVANCE != REVERSAL

The discovery that a historical state depends upon P does not itself authorize
modification of that state.

## State relation

R(P) = R-(P) UNION R+(P)

where:

R-(P) = prior states dependent upon P
R+(P) = prospective states dependent upon P

Any alteration requires independent authorization through UAA and the
applicable constitutional/domain consequence boundary.
