# Predicate Equivalence

For predicates P1 and P2, similarity is not equivalence.

CSI SHALL classify a relationship as:

- IDENTICAL
- NARROWED
- EXPANDED
- PARTIALLY_OVERLAPPING
- DIFFERENT
- UNRESOLVED

A prior institutional holding may only be treated as predicate-identical when
the relevant predicate relationship has been explicitly resolved.

Institutional hierarchy may determine legal control.

Institutional hierarchy does not itself prove factual or semantic equivalence
between predicates.

These are independently represented states.
