# CSI Batch 10 Open Proof Obligations

CSI-B10-PO-001
Prove temporal authority expiration binds all operation-specific UAA decisions.

CSI-B10-PO-002
Prove revoked authority cannot be revived by stale delegation.

CSI-B10-PO-003
Prove simultaneous valid authorities remain conflicted absent explicit precedence basis.

CSI-B10-PO-004
Prove an agent cannot resolve authority collision merely by selecting one actor.

CSI-B10-PO-005
Prove finality remains distinct from validity.

CSI-B10-PO-006
Prove reopening authority remains distinct from original execution authority.

CSI-B10-PO-007
Prove deterministic relevance cannot bypass finality controls.

CSI-B10-PO-008
Prove inherited-state invalidity cannot bypass reopening controls.

CSI-B10-PO-009
Prove reopened state preserves prior final state provenance.

CSI-B10-PO-010
Prove audit bundle digest changes when any constituent execution proof changes.

CSI-B10-PO-011
Prove live audit generation works for both admitted and blocked executions.

CSI-B10-PO-012
Prove temporal, authority-collision, and finality states cannot be silently omitted from final execution proof.
