# CONSTITUTIONAL MECHANISM ONTOLOGY
## Wave 01

F1 — AUTHORITY-VESTING
Authority originates through an identified constitutional source
and is vested in an identified principal or institution.

F2 — AUTHORITY-SEPARATION
One authority class does not silently become another authority class.

F3 — ENUMERATED-SCOPED-POWER
Valid authority remains bounded by granted scope.

F4 — MULTIPARTY-CONCURRENCE
Required independent constitutional actors cannot be replaced
by unilateral action from one participant.

F5 — PROHIBITION-NEGATIVE-CONSTRAINT
Capability cannot defeat an explicit constitutional prohibition.

F6 — JURISDICTION
Authority is valid only inside its constitutionally applicable domain.

F7 — CONTROLLED-MUTATION
Constitutional state may change only through an authorized
amendment or supersession mechanism.
