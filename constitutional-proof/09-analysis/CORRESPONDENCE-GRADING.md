# CONSTITUTIONAL CORRESPONDENCE GRADING STANDARD

C0 — NO CORRESPONDENCE
No demonstrated ORYNTH correspondence.

C1 — CONCEPTUAL
Conceptual resemblance only.

C2 — ARCHITECTURAL
A preexisting ORYNTH mechanism implements the same structural condition.

C3 — FORMAL
A preexisting invariant or proof obligation formally represents
the constitutional mechanism.

C4 — EXECUTABLE
A preexisting proof or executable test demonstrates the constitutional
condition through observable PASS/BLOCK evidence.

G1 — GENUINE GAP
No demonstrated corresponding ORYNTH mechanism.

Executable Constitutional Coverage =
C4 / Total Normalized Constitutional Mechanisms

Formal Constitutional Coverage =
(C3 + C4) / Total Normalized Constitutional Mechanisms

Architectural Constitutional Coverage =
(C2 + C3 + C4) / Total Normalized Constitutional Mechanisms
