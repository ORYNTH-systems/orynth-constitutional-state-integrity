# ORYNTH CONSTITUTIONAL PROOF HYPOTHESES

## H1 — Federal Constitutional Correspondence

ORYNTH contains preexisting executable mechanisms corresponding
to underlying authority-control structures expressed by the
Constitution of the United States.

H1 is not presumed true.

CONSTITUTIONAL SOURCE
→ CLAUSE
→ NORMALIZED MECHANISM
→ PREDICATE
→ PREEXISTING ORYNTH BINDING
→ PROOF
→ EXECUTION RESULT

## H2 — Jurisdictional Generality

A common ORYNTH mechanism set can represent materially distinct
constitutional jurisdictions without collapsing their authority,
scope, sovereignty, or local rule differences.

H2 is reserved for the fifty-state proof program.

## Falsification

Missing correspondence is preserved as a genuine gap.
Constitutional text may not be reinterpreted to manufacture a match.
