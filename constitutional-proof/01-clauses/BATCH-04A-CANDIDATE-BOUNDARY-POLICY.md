# BATCH 04A — CANDIDATE ATOMIC BOUNDARY POLICY

status: ACTIVE

## Purpose

Generate reviewable textual boundary candidates from the 24 verified structural source units.

## Non-Claim

A punctuation boundary is not automatically a constitutional clause boundary.

Batch 04A does not determine constitutional meaning, mechanism class, predicate structure, or ORYNTH correspondence.

## Generation Rule

Candidate boundaries are proposed at semicolons and terminal periods.

Colon boundaries are not automatically split.

## Reconstruction Requirement

Candidate text for every parent source unit must reconstruct the complete parent text modulo boundary whitespace normalization.

No constitutional words may disappear.

No constitutional words may be introduced.

## Adjudication Requirement

Every candidate remains PENDING-ADJUDICATION until Batch 04B.

Batch 04B may merge, retain, or further subdivide candidates according to the frozen clause-boundary rules.

## Separation Invariants

SOURCE != INTERPRETATION

TEXTUAL BOUNDARY != CONSTITUTIONAL MECHANISM

CONSTITUTIONAL MECHANISM != ORYNTH CORRESPONDENCE

CAPABILITY != AUTHORITY
