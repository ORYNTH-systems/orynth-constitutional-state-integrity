# CONSTITUTIONAL CLAUSE BOUNDARY RULES

status: ACTIVE
applies_to: US Constitution Articles I–VII Wave 01

## Purpose

Convert verified structural source units into atomic constitutional source propositions without importing ORYNTH concepts or external doctrine.

## Rule 1 — Source Before Interpretation

SOURCE != INTERPRETATION.

A boundary decision may separate text. It may not infer an ORYNTH mechanism.

## Rule 2 — Preserve Original Text

Every atomic unit must reproduce contiguous source text from its verified parent source unit.

No words may be inserted, deleted, modernized, or paraphrased in the canonical text field.

## Rule 3 — Structural Boundaries Are Evidence

Article and Section boundaries already established by the source are preserved.

## Rule 4 — Internal Boundaries Require Adjudication

A Section may contain multiple independently operative grants, requirements, conditions, prohibitions, exceptions, or concurrence requirements.

These must not be collapsed merely because they occur within one numbered Section.

## Rule 5 — Atomicity Test

A candidate atomic clause should express one independently testable constitutional proposition or one inseparable condition attached to that proposition.

## Rule 6 — Conditions Remain Bound

A condition, exception, threshold, temporal limitation, jurisdictional limitation, or required concurrence must remain textually linked to the proposition it constrains.

## Rule 7 — No Doctrine Yet

Judicial doctrine, case law, historical gloss, and interpretive precedent are excluded from this source-boundary pass.

## Rule 8 — Supersession Deferred

Amendment-based modification or supersession remains UNASSESSED until the Amendments I–XXVII layer is canonized and reconciled.

## Rule 9 — No ORYNTH Crosswalk

ORYNTH mapping is prohibited until constitutional mechanisms and predicates are independently normalized.

## Output

Each adjudicated atomic clause must retain:

- parent source_unit_id
- exact constitutional text
- source locator
- text SHA256
- article
- section
- atomic clause ordinal
- boundary rationale
- supersession status

Only after this layer is frozen may constitutional mechanism normalization begin.
