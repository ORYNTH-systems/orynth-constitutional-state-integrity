# BATCH 04B — CONSTITUTIONAL CLAUSE BOUNDARY ADJUDICATION RULES

status: ACTIVE

## Objective

Determine constitutional atomic boundaries from verified source text without importing ORYNTH architecture or external legal doctrine.

## Permitted Decisions

RETAIN

MERGE-WITH-PREVIOUS

MERGE-WITH-NEXT

FURTHER-SPLIT

## RETAIN

Use when the candidate expresses a source-text proposition that can be evaluated independently without severing a necessary textual condition, exception, threshold, object, or concurrence requirement.

## MERGE

Use when punctuation separated text that remains constitutionally inseparable from the adjacent candidate.

## FURTHER-SPLIT

Use when a punctuation candidate contains multiple independently testable source propositions.

## Conditions

Conditions, exceptions, thresholds, temporal limits, jurisdictional limits, and concurrence requirements must remain bound to the proposition they constrain unless the source independently states them.

## Source Integrity

All final atomic clauses must consist solely of contiguous constitutional source text.

No paraphrase may replace canonical source text.

No constitutional words may be added or removed.

## Doctrine Boundary

Case law, historical interpretation, judicial doctrine, and external constitutional commentary are excluded from this pass.

## Architecture Boundary

ORYNTH terminology, mechanisms, predicates, proofs, and architecture are prohibited from influencing clause-boundary decisions.

## Final Gate

The atomic corpus may freeze only after every candidate has a closed adjudication decision and every final parent reconstructs exactly.
