# VIP Registration Transition Proofs

VIP-REG-PROOF-001

CREATE REGISTERED requires:

IDENTITY_RESOLVED
AND
JURISDICTION_RESOLVED
AND
ELIGIBILITY_RESOLVED_TRUE
AND
AUTHORIZED_REGISTRATION_ACTOR
AND
PROVENANCE_COMPLETE

VIP-REG-PROOF-002

REMOVE REGISTERED requires:

IDENTITY_RESOLVED
AND
APPLICABLE_INELIGIBILITY_PREDICATE_TRUE
AND
SUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE
AND
AUTHORIZED_REMOVAL_ACTOR
AND
PROCEDURAL_CONSTRAINTS_SATISFIED
AND
PROVENANCE_COMPLETE

VIP-REG-PROOF-003

DATABASE_CONFLICT alone cannot satisfy REMOVE.

VIP-REG-PROOF-004

AUTONOMOUS_AGENT_DETECTION alone cannot satisfy REMOVE.

VIP-REG-PROOF-005

RESTORE requires an independently authorized transition.

VIP-REG-PROOF-006

Prior registration does not itself prove continuing eligibility.

VIP-REG-PROOF-007

Later relevance does not itself prove prior registration invalidity.
