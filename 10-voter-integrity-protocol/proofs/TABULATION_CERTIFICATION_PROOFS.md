# VIP Tabulation and Certification Proofs

VIP-TAB-PROOF-001

Only an admitted ballot may produce tabulation effect.

VIP-TAB-PROOF-002

One admitted ballot may not produce duplicate tabulation effect.

VIP-TAB-PROOF-003

Tabulation capability does not establish tabulation authority.

VIP-CERT-PROOF-001

Certification requires an explicitly authorized certifying actor.

VIP-CERT-PROOF-002

Possession of records does not establish certification authority.

VIP-CERT-PROOF-003

Certification must bind to an admitted canvass/tabulation state.

VIP-CERT-PROOF-004

Certification preserves provenance.

VIP-CERT-PROOF-005

Finality does not erase evidence or authority lineage.
