## Execution Principle

Consequential execution requires:

VALID SOURCE
AND
TRUE PREDICATE
AND
VALID JURISDICTION
AND
VALID AUTHORITY
AND
SUFFICIENT EVIDENCE

No individual condition substitutes for another.

## Constitutional Boundary

The United States Constitution remains upstream source.

The executable kernel consumes constitutional source mappings.

It does not modify constitutional source.

## Agentic Boundary

Agents may participate in delegated execution.

Agentic capability does not create authority.

Delegation cannot exceed principal authority.

## Deterministic Relevance

Dependency relevance may propagate backward and forward.

Relevance does not authorize state alteration.
